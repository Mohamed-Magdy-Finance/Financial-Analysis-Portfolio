#  Integrated 3-Statement Financial Model

A sophisticated financial model that dynamically links the three core financial statements: the Income Statement, Balance Sheet, and Cash Flow Statement. This model is designed to provide a holistic view of a company's financial health and operational performance.

---

##  Model Architecture
This project demonstrates the ability to build a fully integrated financial system where any change in operational assumptions flows seamlessly through all financial reports.

### Key Financial Statements included:
* **Income Statement:** Tracking revenue, COGS, operating expenses, and net income.
* **Balance Sheet:** Managing assets, liabilities, and equity, ensuring the fundamental accounting equation always balances.
* **Cash Flow Statement:** Reconciling net income to cash through operating, investing, and financing activities.

---

##  Core Functionalities
* **Dynamic Linking:** The three statements are interconnected; for example, Net Income flows into Retained Earnings, and Depreciation affects both the Income Statement and the Cash Flow.
* **Forecasting Logic:** Built-in projections for future periods based on growth and margin assumptions.
* **Debt & Interest Schedules:** (If applicable) Integration of debt repayments and interest expense calculations.
