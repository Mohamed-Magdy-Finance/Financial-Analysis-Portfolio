##  Model Overview
This project demonstrates advanced financial modeling skills by linking operational drivers (like web traffic) to financial outcomes (Income Statement). It provides a month-by-month breakdown of a startup's first year of operations.

### Key Components:
* **Revenue Drivers:** Detailed analysis of Organic vs. Paid traffic and their impact on total revenue.
* **Growth Metrics:** Monthly tracking of user acquisition, starting from 1,500 organic visitors and scaling to over 10,000.
* **Expense Management:** Classification of costs into:
    * **Fixed Costs:** General, administrative, and other overheads (e.g., $1,900/month initially).
    * **Variable Costs:** Costs that scale directly with traffic and sales volume.

---

##  Technical Skills Applied:
* **Financial Modeling:** Building a bottom-up forecast from operational assumptions.
* **Trend & Variance Analysis:** Projecting growth trends for traffic and revenue over a 12-month period.
* **Excel Engineering:** Advanced formulas to automate total cost calculations and profit margins.
* **FP&A:** Integrated financial planning and analysis for decision support.

---

##  Repository Structure:
* `Start-Startup-Financial-Model.xlsx`: The core interactive financial model.
* `Model_Analysis.csv`: Raw data extract used for reporting.

---

##  How to Use:
1. Download the `Start-Startup-Financial-Model.xlsx` file.
2. Review the **Assumptions** section to adjust Organic or Paid traffic numbers.
3. Observe how the **Total Costs** and **Revenue** update automatically across the 12-month forecast.
