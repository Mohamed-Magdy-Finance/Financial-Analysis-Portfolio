#  Leveraged Buyout (LBO) Analysis Model

This model is a sophisticated private equity tool designed to evaluate the acquisition of a company using a significant amount of borrowed funds. It calculates the internal rate of return (IRR) and money-on-money (MoM) multiples for investors.

---

##  Model Architecture
The **Basic-LBO-Modeling.xlsx** provides a structured, multi-step approach to transaction analysis, covering everything from entry assumptions to exit returns.

### Core Modeling Steps:
1. **Entry Valuation:** Setting the purchase enterprise value based on LTM EBITDA multiples (e.g., 10.0x entry multiple).
2. **Transaction Assumptions:** Factoring in critical deal costs such as transaction fees, financing fees, and minimum cash requirements.
3. **Sources & Uses:** (Implicit in structure) Identifying where the capital comes from (Debt vs. Equity) and how it is applied to the purchase.
4. **Debt Schedule:** Tracking the mandatory and optional repayment of debt over the investment horizon.
5. **Returns Analysis:** Calculating the final IRR and cash-on-cash multiples based on the exit valuation.

---

##  Key Financial Logic:
* **Operating Leverage:** Modeling how operational improvements and debt paydown drive equity value growth.
* **Capital Structure Optimization:** Analyzing the impact of different debt-to-equity ratios on investor returns.
* **Financing Fees Amortization:** Detailed accounting for financing costs over the life of the loan (e.g., 7-year amortization).

---

##  Technical Skills Applied:
* **Private Equity Modeling:** Understanding the mechanics of leveraged transactions.
* **Complex Debt Sculpting:** Managing senior and subordinated debt layers within a financial model.
* **Transaction Structuring:** Ability to model fees, minimum cash balances, and purchase price adjustments.
