#  Intrinsic Valuation: Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) Model

This model provides a detailed framework for calculating the intrinsic value of a business by projecting future free cash flows and discounting them back to their present value using a specific discount rate (WACC).

---

##  Model Architecture
The **DCFguide.xlsx** file serves as a comprehensive guide and calculator for various DCF scenarios, ensuring accuracy in long-term valuation assessments.

### Key Valuation Components:
* **Annuity Cash Flows:** Calculating present value for stable, consistent cash flows over a fixed period.
* **Variable Cash Flows:** Modeling fluctuating cash flows across different periods to reflect realistic business cycles.
* **Sum of Present Values:** Aggregating discounted future cash flows to determine the total Enterprise Value.
* **Unlevered Free Cash Flows (UFCF):** Advanced application focusing on Apple's cash flow structure to evaluate core business performance.

---

##  Core Financial Logic:
* **Time Value of Money (TVM):** Applying discount rates (e.g., 25% baseline) to future earnings to account for risk and opportunity cost.
* **Period-by-Period Discounting:** Precision calculation of present value for each specific year in the forecast.
* **Intrinsic Value Estimation:** Moving beyond market sentiment to find the "true" value of the company based on its cash-generating power.

---

##  Technical Skills Applied:
* **Advanced Financial Mathematics:** Mastery of PV and NPV formulas in complex scenarios.
* **Strategic Valuation:** Ability to perform deep-dive intrinsic analysis for blue-chip companies like Apple.
* **Sensitivity Analysis:** Evaluating how changes in the discount rate (r) impact the final valuation.
