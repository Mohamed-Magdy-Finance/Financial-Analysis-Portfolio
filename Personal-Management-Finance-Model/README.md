# Personal Finance Management & Budgeting Model 

An advanced Excel-based financial tool designed to track income, manage expenses, and analyze personal net worth dynamics. This model serves as a practical application of financial planning and analysis (FP&A) principles applied to personal wealth management.

## 🔹 Features & Functionalities

* **Integrated Financial Dashboard:** A centralized visual hub providing a high-level overview of year-to-date (YTD) performance.
* **Dynamic Income Tracking:** Detailed monitoring of various income streams including Base Salary, Bonuses, Side Hustles, and Investment returns.
* **Expense Analysis:** Granular tracking of monthly expenditures across categories such as Rent, Utilities, Transport, Groceries, and Leisure.
* **Growth Metrics:** Built-in Month-over-Month (MoM %) growth analysis to track financial progress and volatility.
* **Transaction Ledger:** A structured 'Inputs' sheet for logging daily transactions with automated category mapping.

##  Technical Specifications

* **Automated Summaries:** Utilizes advanced Excel formulas to aggregate data from raw inputs to the main dashboard.
* **Reconciliation:** Designed to ensure all cash inflows and outflows are accounted for in the ending cash balance.
* **Scalability:** The model structure allows for easy expansion to include more categories or fiscal years.

##  Project Structure

* **Dashboard Sheet:** The primary analytical layer showing monthly totals and YTD summaries.
* **Inputs Sheet:** The data entry layer for recording actual income and expenses with descriptions.

##  How to Use

1. Clone the repository to your local machine.
2. Open `START-Personal-Budgeting-File_v2.xlsx`.
3. Start entering your transactions in the **Inputs** tab.
4. Monitor your financial health through the **Dashboard**.
