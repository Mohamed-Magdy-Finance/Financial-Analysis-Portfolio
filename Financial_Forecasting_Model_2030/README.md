

Strategic Financial Forecasting & Scenario Analysis System
This professional Excel model is engineered for long-term strategic planning (2026-2030). It empowers decision-makers to evaluate different business outcomes through a dynamic scenario-based engine, providing deep insights into future profitability and cost structures.

1. Dynamic Scenario & Forecast Engine
2. Operational Cost Structure (COGS)
3. Long-term Profitability Analysis
   
 Project Description
The system is designed to provide a "forward-looking" view of the business. Unlike traditional tracking tools, this model focuses on Strategic Projections, allowing you to simulate how changes in revenue or COGS affect your bottom line over half a decade.

 Core Functionalities
Scenario Manager: A centralized control (Cell K2) to switch between business cases instantly.

5-Year Horizon: Structured forecasting starting from Year 1 (2026) up to Year 5 (2030).

Margin Monitoring: Real-time calculation of Gross Profit Margins, maintaining a 78% efficiency in the baseline.

Automated Aggregation: Seamless integration of Revenue and Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) into a final summary.
